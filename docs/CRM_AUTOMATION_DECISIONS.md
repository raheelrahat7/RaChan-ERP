# CRM assignment and follow-up tracking

- Users with Manage CRM permission may assign or unassign unconverted leads to organization members. Cross-organization assignment is rejected and changes are audited.
- Activities with a due date are scheduled follow-ups. The CRM page lists incomplete follow-ups for unconverted leads in due-date order, with overdue indicators.
- Managers may complete scheduled follow-ups once. Completion is timestamped and audited; completed items remain in activity history.
- Converted leads cannot be reassigned or receive new follow-ups. Existing records are retained.
- CRM dashboard alerts and daily in-app digest categories cover overdue follow-ups and open follow-ups due later today. Completed activities and converted leads are excluded. The existing organization preferences control delivery, and daily event keys prevent duplicate notifications for each user/category/date.
- Delivery follows the existing organization-wide digest to organization members, rather than an assignee-only channel. The existing Laravel schedule runs generation at 08:00; the deployment must run Laravel's scheduler. No email or external message delivery is introduced.
- This increment reuses existing CRM fields. Pipeline automation remains a subsequent increment.
- The approved dynamic pipeline foundation is implemented separately and documented in [CRM_DYNAMIC_PIPELINE_PLAN.md](CRM_DYNAMIC_PIPELINE_PLAN.md). Configuration is restricted to organization owners/administrators; existing Manage CRM controls manual movement and explicit conversion. Entering Lost requires a pipeline-owned active reason; reopening clears the current reason and retains historical snapshots. Explicit conversion records a move to an active Won stage when necessary, while manual Won movement does not create customers.
- Pipeline changes do not automatically assign users or pause/close follow-ups. Existing reminder behavior is preserved. The later approved stage-entry notification increment below adds only a configurable in-app message to the assignee.

- The approved pipeline board uses the same server movement action as the lead list. Drops open a confirmation form with required reasons for Lost; keyboard/touch users use Move. No optimistic state change occurs before server acceptance. Pipeline/stage/assignee filtering applies to board cards, with visible counts distinguished from scoped totals.

- Configurable stage entry rules now restrict source stages, organization roles and required existing lead fields. Unrestricted defaults preserve existing behavior. Manual movement, reopening, creation requirements and explicit conversion use the same CRM rule service; conversion cannot bypass Won role/field requirements. Rule changes and lead-details edits are audited. Board/list explanations come from the server, and referenced source stages cannot be deleted while rules use them.

- Pipeline reporting is read-only and gated by CRM view permission. Outcomes are distinct lead states at the end-date cutoff; period-created leads and explicit conversions are separate metrics. Win rate uses Won/(Won+Lost); new-lead conversion uses period-created leads converted during that period. Assignee attribution is current, historical labels are preserved, and open-stage time excludes terminal waiting and unobserved pre-tracking time. Missing history is explicitly untracked.
- The first visibility increment scoped Members and Viewers to their assigned leads across lead views, related activity/follow-up lists, pipeline reports, dashboard CRM counts and lead exports. Its Manager-wide exception was superseded by the hierarchy decision below.
- The approved hierarchy increment supersedes the initial Manager-wide exception: Managers see their own assigned leads plus explicitly granted department/subdepartment/organization view scopes. Owners and Administrators see all leads. Any member may receive extra view scopes without gaining CRM write permission. Team membership alone never exposes a colleague's leads. Scope applies to direct CRM mutations by Managers as well as read endpoints; organization-wide view grants include unassigned leads.
- Cross-pipeline lead transfers require explicit destination pipeline/stage selection and confirmation. They enforce destination entry rules and Lost reasons, preserve history and do not automatically convert a customer, assign an agent or change follow-ups. The later stage-entry notification rule also applies to transfers into configured stages. Pipeline reports use the recorded pipeline at the selected cutoff for filtered outcomes.
- Owners/Administrators may enable an in-app assignee notification per stage. Each committed entry event freezes the enabled flag and recipient in history; delivery is deduplicated by history ID and audited. Current membership and assignment are checked before delivery. Daily digest preferences are separate. No email, webhook, task creation, customer conversion or follow-up changes are triggered.
- The separately approved follow-up increment lets Owners/Administrators set a stage-entry task due 0–365 days after entry. An enabled rule creates one task per committed entry for an assigned, unconverted lead; the history reference deduplicates replay. Current organization membership and assignment are checked before creation. Existing follow-up lists and alerts surface the task, and it follows the lead's current assignee. Rule changes and task creation are audited. No email, webhook, assignment, conversion or automatic completion is triggered.
- The approved assignment increment lets Owners/Administrators select a round-robin member pool for each stage. The initial stage applies on new lead creation; other stages apply when an unassigned lead enters. Existing assignees, including restricted creators assigned to themselves, stay unchanged. The pool skips members who have left the organization and leaves the lead unassigned when none remain. Assignment is transactional and precedes the stage history snapshot and its notification/follow-up listeners. Explicit customer conversion does not run assignment.
- The later routing extension gives Meta form ID/name, campaign, and project matches precedence over the stage pool. Routes target selected members or active hierarchy units. An agent must check in each local day and stay below the configured active-lead quota. Unassignable leads enter an audited hold queue, notify Owners/Administrators only, and retry in arrival order. Meta Lead Ads intake uses a signed webhook, encrypted Page token, Redis job, idempotent Meta lead ID, and keeps partial leads for correction. See [CRM_ASSIGNMENT_ROUTING_AND_META.md](CRM_ASSIGNMENT_ROUTING_AND_META.md).
- The optional follow-up reminder setting sends one in-app notice to a lead's current organization assignee at 08:00 in the organization's timezone, 1–30 local days before an open follow-up's due date. The default is off. Completed follow-ups, converted or unassigned leads, and former organization members are excluded. The scheduled job runs every 15 minutes during the local 08:00 hour, and a per-user task/date event key prevents duplicate notices. Owners/Administrators configure the lead time in CRM assignment routing. Reminders do not complete tasks or message external channels.
