# Internal chat and voice-call increment

The ERP now has organization-scoped workspace, direct, and group chat rooms. Any current organization member can participate in the workspace, open a direct conversation with another current member, or create a group with at least two colleagues. Direct and group history is visible only to room members; removing organization membership immediately removes access. Message bodies are encrypted at rest. The chat page refreshes messages through scoped HTTP polling because the current stack has no WebSocket broadcaster.

Voice calling is available in direct chats only. Browser WebRTC carries audio between participants; Z1 stores short-lived encrypted offer, answer, and ICE signaling until the call ends. Z1 does not record audio. Browser microphone permission and a secure context are required. `CHAT_ICE_SERVERS_JSON` in `.env` may supply STUN/TURN server definitions; without configured servers, calls across different networks may fail even though local-network calling works. A production TURN service and physical-device acceptance are still required for reliable external-network calling.

Only organization owners and administrators can download the complete organization chat transcript, including direct messages, as explicitly selected by the owner. Ordinary staff cannot download chat history. Lead and lead-activity exports remain separately controlled by the owner-managed CRM export grant, and a delegate sees only leads they are already allowed to view. Chat export is audited and spreadsheet-safe; no chat content is inserted into the CRM lead export because internal conversations are not linked to individual leads.

Validation: run `php artisan test tests/Feature/Chat/InternalChatTest.php`, the CRM lead import/export tests, `vendor/bin/phpstan analyse`, `npm run types:check`, `npm run test:frontend`, and `npm run build`. Voice-call signaling has automated permission tests; physical browser-to-browser media connectivity still needs two-device acceptance with the intended TURN configuration.
