# CRM Settings backend continuation

The authenticated JSON API exposes organization-scoped configuration at `GET/POST /organization/crm-catalog/{kind}` and `PUT /organization/crm-catalog/{kind}/{record}`. Supported kinds are `taxes`, `units`, `detail-templates`, `company-details`, `mailboxes`, and `products`. All writes require `manageSettings`; each record has an immutable organization/kind/code, editable name/settings/position, and an `active` flag for archiving. Taxes accept a percentage rate and description; units accept a symbol and decimal precision; templates accept a `contact` or `company` entity and field keys; company details accept legal/contact identity; mailboxes accept email, display name, and reply-to metadata; products accept price/currency and optional active organization unit/tax references.

`GET/POST /crm/leads/{lead}/products`, `PUT/DELETE /crm/leads/{lead}/products/{line}` manage lead product lines with quantity, unit price, and currency. Read access follows current lead visibility; writes also require CRM edit permission. The GET response includes linked estimate workflow records visible under both lead and finance access. `POST /reference-workflows` can accept `lead_id` only for an estimate; this link is immutable, organization validated, and included in replay checking. The create response includes database-generated `version` immediately. Deal `stageCounts` include a two-decimal `amount` sum over the current filtered deals and the actor's amount scope. Where the actor has no amount access, `amount` is `null`.

The tax catalog and product prices are editable reference data. They do not change invoice, VAT, accounting, or estimate calculations automatically. Mailbox records hold sender metadata only; outbound delivery and provider credentials remain unconfigured by the user's choice. Contact/company templates are stored field selections; UI layout rendering remains a frontend task.
