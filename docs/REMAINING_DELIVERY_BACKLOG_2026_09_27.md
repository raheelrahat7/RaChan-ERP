The user authorized completion of remaining work on 2026-09-27. Earlier pilot checkpoints remain historical evidence, not evidence that the broader roadmap is complete.

## Approved implementation boundaries

- Stock transfers use linked atomic ledger legs, prohibit negative stock and require paired reasoned corrections. Valuation pools weighted-average value per part across the organization, uses explicitly recorded receipt total costs and one currency per part, preserves value on transfers and restores original issue costs on returns. Missing/inconsistent costs are visibly unvalued. No automatic journal posting.
- AMC covers dated properties/equipment and service limits without recurring invoices. Linking a job reserves a visit; cancellation releases it, reopening retains that visit. Managers may exceed limits with a recorded reason.
- Invited tenants and landlords have explicit independent customer grants. Tenants read linked leases/invoices and submit service requests; landlords read owned properties, owner statements and service summaries. Invitations do not grant employee membership.
- Construction uses gross BOQ rates and fixed approved claim amounts. Owners approve claims submitted by another user; finance chooses existing VAT/expense/capital treatment and creates a draft bill, then uses the normal finance posting workflow.
- Vendor cash returns record money received from the vendor after a supplier credit creates an actual overpayment. Finance requests, a different owner approves, and the receipt debits payment clearing and credits accounts payable. Capacity is checked at the receipt date and today.
- Documents archive with a reason and retain all versions. Permanent deletion remains disabled until a retention policy is approved.
- General API tokens expire within 90 days, are revocable and read-only. Organization/module permissions and technician job assignments are checked on each request. No finance/workflow mutation endpoints.
- Scheduled PDF/XLSX reports are private in-app deliveries to their creator. Permissions are checked at generation and download; reassignment of an included job blocks downloading the old artifact. Daily/weekly times use the organization timezone. Missed occurrences coalesce into one current snapshot. Reports cap at 1000 jobs and 10000 export rows; excessive size fails visibly rather than truncating.
- External providers/hosting remain unselected. Build provider-neutral adapters and local validation; do not infer live activation or send external messages.

The owner also selected Arabic customer-portal and technician workflows first; specialized finance wording awaits accountant review. See [Arabic scope](ARABIC_FIRST_RELEASE_SCOPE.md) and [provider contracts](PROVIDER_NEUTRAL_CONTRACTS.md).
